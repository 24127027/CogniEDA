Evaluate whether the accumulated execution results satisfy the original task instruction.

Rules:
- verdict=satisfied: all critical steps succeeded and their combined outputs
  directly answer the task instruction. Provide a concise summary of findings.
- verdict=needs_revision: one or more steps failed, or the combined outputs are
  incomplete. Provide clear revision_feedback describing exactly what is missing
  or incorrect so that planning can produce a corrective plan. If a builtin tool failed
  due to its constraints (like exceeding max_groups) or doesn't exist, explicitly tell
  the planner to use execution_type=code_generation.
- verdict=unfeasible: the task cannot be resolved with the available dataset
  columns and bounded operations, regardless of retries. Explain why.
- Do not interpret results scientifically, claim discoveries, evaluate hypotheses,
  or produce prose beyond what is needed to describe completeness.
- reference only data that appears in the supplied execution_results payloads.
